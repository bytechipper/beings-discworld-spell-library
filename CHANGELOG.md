# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial offline library of 116 current Discworld spells.
- Searchable A–Z list with abbreviations, Wizard/Witch badges, and spell types.
- Detail popups with full wiki articles, learning sources, spell-book locations, components, GP, size, skills, and bonus chart estimates.
- Reproducible package and automated data, runtime, and browser tests.
